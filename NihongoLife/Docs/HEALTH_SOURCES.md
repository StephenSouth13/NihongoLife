# Health content — sources

`Resources/Health/illnesses.json` (illnesses, treatment, medicines), the clinic interviews in
`Resources/Progression/careers.json` and the handbook's "becoming a doctor" page follow these public sources
(checked 2026-10-27). Game times are compressed (1 game hour = 1 real minute); the rules are kept.

| Topic | What the game uses | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Colds are viral; antibiotics don't work | Cold medicine only relieves; the doctor gives no antibiotics; the career's interview asks it | AMR Clinical Reference Center (NCGM), patient leaflet after the MHLW "Manual of Antimicrobial Stewardship": https://amr.ncgm.go.jp/pdf/20230412_kanbo.pdf |
| Flu antivirals work best within 48 h | Clinic treatment shortens flu 80 % in the first 48 game hours, 30 % later | Takamatsu City flu leaflet: https://www.city.takamatsu.kagawa.jp/kurashi/kenkou/kansensho/joho/chuikanki.files/202409influenza.pdf ; pharmacy leaflet: https://www.japha.jp/doc/byoki/033.pdf |
| Flu: stay home (5 days after onset and 2 days after the fever) | `stayHome`: working with flu costs reputation; handbook text | School Health and Safety Act enforcement rules, as summarised by Oita University health centre: https://www.hc.oita-u.ac.jp/info/インフルエンザと診断された場合の出席停止期間 |
| Heatstroke first aid | Shade, cool neck/armpits/groin, oral rehydration solution, call 119 when confused; a drip at the clinic | MHLW heatstroke page: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/kenkou/nettyuu/nettyuu_taisaku/happen.html ; Tokyo Fire Department: https://www.tfd.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/content/000026712.pdf |
| Hay fever prevalence (42.5 %, cedar 38.8 %, 2019) | Handbook text; masks prevent, antihistamines relieve (older ones make you drowsy) | Cabinet Secretariat pollen-measures material: https://www.cas.go.jp/jp/seisaku/kafun/dai1/02siryo2.pdf |
| Delayed-onset muscle soreness | Heals with rest and time; patches relieve; cool first, warm after 2–3 days | Vanderbilt Health: https://healthlibrary.vanderbilthealth.com/85,P00918 ; meta-analysis: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34636405/ |
| Becoming a doctor in Japan | Medical school 6 years → national exam (about 90 % pass) → residency ≥ 2 years → specialty training about 3 years in one of 19 basic fields | https://www.agaroot.jp/agaroot-medical/column/to-become-a-doctor/ ; MHLW specialist-system material: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/file/05-Shingikai-10801000-Iseikyoku-Soumuka/0000163149.pdf |
| Hand, foot and mouth disease (children, summer; no antibiotics; no fixed school-exclusion period) | `hand_foot_mouth` and `case_hfmd`: fluids, soft cool food, a fever reducer when needed; red flags are poor drinking, high fever and drowsiness | Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) leaflet on hand-foot-mouth disease and the National Center for Child Health and Development (NCCHD) page 手足口病, via https://www.mhlw.go.jp/ and https://www.ncchd.go.jp/ ; JIHS infectious disease information https://id-info.jihs.go.jp/ |
| Epidemic keratoconjunctivitis (virus; wash hands, stay away until it can no longer spread) | `conjunctivitis` and `case_conj`: eye drops from the eye doctor, hand washing, no towel sharing; no antibiotics for a virus | Japan Pediatric Society https://www.jpeds.or.jp/ ; MHLW https://www.mhlw.go.jp/ ; School Health and Safety Act enforcement rules (second-class infectious disease, as the doctor decides) |
| Strep throat (group A streptococcus): bacteria, so antibiotics, taken for the full 10 days | `strep_throat` and `case_strep`: the one illness in the clinic where an antibiotic is the right answer; stopping early risks rheumatic fever and kidney inflammation | MHLW "Manual of Antimicrobial Stewardship" (抗微生物薬適正使用の手引き) https://www.mhlw.go.jp/ ; Japan Pediatric Society https://www.jpeds.or.jp/ ; NCCHD 溶連菌感染症 https://www.ncchd.go.jp/ |
| Acute otitis media in children: pain relief and watchful waiting first, antibiotics when it worsens | `otitis_media` and `case_otitis`: pain reliever and a re-check; antibiotics only if worse | Japan Otological Society, Clinical Practice Guidelines for Acute Otitis Media in Children (小児急性中耳炎診療ガイドライン) https://www.otology.gr.jp/ |
| Heat rash (miliaria): keep cool, wash sweat off, ointment if it is bad | `heat_rash` and `case_heatrash`; heat-illness prevention shared with the heatstroke row | Japanese Dermatological Association Q&A on あせも https://www.dermatol.or.jp/ ; MHLW heatstroke page (see the heatstroke row) |
| Toothache: see a dentist; painkillers only hold the pain; caries do not heal alone | `toothache` and `case_toothache`: the clinic refers the patient to a dentist (歯科) | Japan Dental Association (8020 campaign and caries prevention) https://www.jda.or.jp/ ; MHLW https://www.mhlw.go.jp/ |
| Medical specialties (診療科) | `specialty*` fields of `Resources/Clinic/medical_training.json`: each case is shown with the specialty that treats it (paediatrics, ENT, ophthalmology, dermatology, dentistry ...) | MHLW specialist-system material (see the "becoming a doctor" row) |

The game is not medical advice; the handbook says to see a doctor, and to call 119 in an emergency.
